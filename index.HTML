About Us

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

SEPAL International Private School System is a modern, co-educational smart school serving students from Playgroup through Grade 5, rooted in Pakistani values and equipped with world-class technology.

🎯 Our Vision

To be Pakistan's leading modern school producing confident critical thinkers and compassionate global citizens — rooted in Pakistani values.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide every child an innovative, inclusive, and technology-enhanced education that develops intellect, character, creativity, and leadership.

Founded with the vision of transforming Pakistani education, SEPAL International combines internationally recognized frameworks like EYFS with Pakistan's curriculum standards. Our smart classrooms, qualified faculty, and strong parent engagement model make us a school that families trust and students love.

Language of instruction: English (Urdu as second language) · School hours: 8:00 AM — 2:00 PM · Campus: Pakistan

Teaching Methodology

Inquiry-Based Learning
Students explore real questions rather than passively receiving answers — developing critical thinking from early on.
Project-Based Learning
Monthly cross-subject projects with real-world connections foster creativity, collaboration, and deep understanding.
Differentiated Instruction
Three ability tracks — support, core, and challenge — ensure every child is appropriately stretched and supported.
Flipped Learning (Gr. 4–5)
Short video lessons assigned at home; class time is used for application, discussion, and deeper exploration.
EdTech

Smart Learning Technology

Technology that enhances, personalizes, and deepens every learning experience — built for Pakistan's context.

75" 4K Interactive Smart Board

Touchscreen digital pen board with pre-loaded lesson plans, subject apps, and grading tools — replaces the traditional blackboard entirely.

Student Tablets (Shared)

1 tablet per 2 students in Grades 3–5 for guided digital learning sessions with child-safe filtered internet access.

High-Speed Filtered WiFi

Fiber internet with Pakistan-safe content filtering — no social media, no inappropriate content, full digital citizenship compliance.

CCTV Safety Monitoring

Full campus CCTV coverage accessible by the principal only — ensuring every child's safety throughout the school day.

Live Parent Attendance Alerts

SMS notification when your child enters and leaves school every day — real-time peace of mind for every parent.

Admissions 2025–26

Admissions are open year-round. Major intake in July–August. Start your child's journey with us today.

Admission Process

1

Enquire Online

Fill in the enquiry form or WhatsApp us. Our admissions team responds within 24 hours.

2

Age Verification

Submit B-Form / Birth Certificate for age verification. No test required for PG, Nursery, KG.

3

School Visit

Book a guided campus tour. Meet the class teacher and see our smart classrooms in action.

4

Transfer Records (Gr. 1–5)

Submit previous school reports and records for a brief readiness check with our VP Academic.

5

Admission Confirmed

Pay registration fee, sign enrolment contract, and receive the welcome package with diary and uniform guide.
